PART 7: TIPS FOR COURT PRESENTATION

  • Be concise: Present your case clearly and without emotion
  • Stick to facts: Bring evidence to support every claim
  • Bring originals: Court requires originals, provide copies to all parties
  • Practice: Rehearse your statement before the hearing
  • Be respectful: Address the judge as "Your Honor"
  • Bring witnesses: Have them wait outside until called
  • Know your remedy: Clearly state the amount you are seeking
